## Diet for Brain Health

 

**Sources:**

- Dale Bredesen - *The End of Alzheimer's*

- Lisa Mosconi - *Brain Food*

- Dean & Ayesha Sherzai - *The Alzheimer's Solution*

- Casey Means - *Good Energy*

 

---

 

**The consensus: Eat a plant-forward diet.**

 

Bredesen, Mosconi, and Sherzai agree: fill most of your plate with plants.  

 

**Build meals around:**

- Leafy greens (spinach, kale, arugula)

- Cruciferous vegetables (cauliflower, kale, brocolli, brussel sprouts)
 
- Colorful vegetables
- Beans and legumes

- Nuts (walnuts, almonds, macadamia, pecans)

- Olive oil

- Avocado

**Fruits (in moderation):**
- Prefer low-glycemic fruit: berries, tart cherries, grapefruit, lemons/limes.
- Or other fruits around workouts.
- 
**Protein**

- Wild-caught fish 2-3x/week.  e.g. sardines.

- Pasture-raised eggs

- Other meats in moderation.

 

**Debated**

- *Whole grains:* Mosconi and Sherzai say they're fine; Bredesen and Means say avoid entirely (low grade inflammation).

- *Dairy:* Mosconi and Sherzai allow some; Bredesen says avoid entirely (low grade inflammation).

 

**Minimize or avoid:**

- Sugar and sweets

- Artificial sweeteners

- Processed/packaged foods

- Cured and processed meats (bacon, deli meat, hot dogs)

- Large quantities of red meat

- Seed/vegetable oils (canola, soybean, corn oil)

- High-mercury fish (tuna, swordfish, king mackerel)

 

**Meal Timing:**

- Keep your eating window to 8-12 hours a day.  12 is enough to be a real benefit.  

- No food for 2-3 hours before bed

 

**Ketosis:**

- Bredesen recommends 75% of calories from fat to achieve a mild ketosis and so get the extra brain fuel.  Austin does 80% here.   Means is also pro-ketogenic.  Mosconi and Sherzai are of the low-fat school.

 

**High Fat vs. Low Fat** - There seems to be a lot of good evidence for a near-vegan, low-fat, plant-forward diet.  There is also a lot of good evidence for a high-fat, low-carb diet.  One thing these share in common is avoidance of empty carbs, processed foods, unhealthy fats and oils, etc.

 

**One simple rule:** Real food, mostly plants.
